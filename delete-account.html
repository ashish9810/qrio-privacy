Delete your Qrio account

Last updated: 9 October 2026

You can delete your Qrio account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Qrio and tap the profile icon at the top right.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us to do it

If you can no longer open the app, email info.ak.ashish@gmail.com from the email address or phone number you signed up with, and write “Delete my Qrio account”. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone number or email).
  • Your preferences and notification token.
  • Your likes, follows, comments and watch history.
  • Any creator application you sent from the app, including the Instagram handle and phone number on it.
  • Your sign-in account.

What we may keep

Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you. Reports you made about content may be kept for safety reasons without your name attached.
