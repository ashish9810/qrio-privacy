Community Guidelines

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Qrio is for learning and good conversation. These rules keep the comments useful and safe for everyone.

Do

  • Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
  • Add something: a question, a fact, a different view.
  • Use the report button when something looks wrong.

Do not

  • Harass or hate. No abuse, threats, or attacks on people for their religion, caste, race, gender, sexuality, disability, nationality or similar.
  • Incite violence or praise violence or terrorism.
  • Spread deliberate misinformation or impersonate someone.
  • Spam or promote. No ads, links to scams, or repeated posts.
  • Share private information about other people.
  • Post sexual, graphic or illegal content.

Politics and sensitive topics

Many Qrio videos cover geopolitics and other topics people feel strongly about. Strong views are fine. Abuse is not.

What happens when rules are broken

Reported comments are reviewed. We may remove a comment, warn you, or suspend your account. You can block anyone, and you will stop seeing their comments.

Report a problem

Use Report in the app, or email info.ak.ashish@gmail.com.
